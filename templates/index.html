About

This app simulates three Zürich bus lines. Each line publishes GPS checkpoints to a Kafka topic every 500 ms, and the map below updates live as new positions arrive over a server-sent event stream.

Data pipeline

1 3 Python producers generate coordinates
↓
2 Kafka topic busdata001
↓
3 Flask consumer
↓
4 Leaflet map, live in browser
